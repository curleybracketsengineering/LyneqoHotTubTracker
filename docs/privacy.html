Privacy Policy

No account with us. Readings stay in your iCloud.

Overview

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Lyneqo Tub is provided by Curley Brackets Engineering. This policy explains how the app handles data.

Data collection

Lyneqo Tub does not require an account with Curley Brackets Engineering and does not collect personal information for us.

  • No account registration is required.
  • No personal information is sent to Curley Brackets Engineering.
  • No advertising or tracking is included.
  • We do not operate our own servers for your water readings.

What you enter

Readings, treatments, usage, reminders and the target ranges you save are stored on your device. If iCloud is available on that device, the app syncs this information through your personal iCloud account so it can appear on your other Apple devices. That sync is handled by Apple. Curley Brackets Engineering cannot read it.

Notifications and files

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders on your device for water tests and care tasks. You can also export or import a CSV backup yourself. Those files stay where you save them.
